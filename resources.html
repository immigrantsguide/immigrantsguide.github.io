📚 U.S. Immigration Forms & Visa Resources

A practical directory of major immigrant, nonimmigrant, work, visitor, humanitarian, religious and special immigration categories.

Government fees change. Always confirm the current fee and form edition on USCIS or the Department of State before filing.

Important: A visa category is not always the same thing as a USCIS form. Some cases use Department of State forms (such as DS-160 or DS-260), while USCIS uses Form I-series applications and petitions. Fees shown below are official-source reference amounts and may have category-specific exceptions.
