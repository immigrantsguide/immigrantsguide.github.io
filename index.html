U.S. Visa Categories, Fees & Forms

Quick reference to major U.S. visa classifications, common government forms, and current Department of State / USCIS fees. Use the official links before filing because fees and filing requirements can change.

🟢 Immigrant Visas

Permanent immigration and green-card pathways.
Family-Based: IR / CR / FIR1/CR1 spouses; IR2/CR2 children; IR5 parents; F1, F2A, F2B, F3, F4 family-preference categories.DOS immigrant visa processing: $325 per person for immediate-relative/family-preference cases. I-130: $625 online / $675 paper.
↓ Form I-130↗ DS-260 Online↗ Immigrant Visa Process
Employment-Based: EB-1, EB-2, EB-3, EB-4, EB-5Priority workers; advanced-degree / exceptional-ability; professionals/skilled/other workers; special immigrants; immigrant investors.DOS employment-based immigrant visa processing: $345 per person. Form I-140: $715.
↓ Form I-140↗ EB Visa Info
Special / Other Immigrant CategoriesDV diversity immigrants, SB returning residents, certain SI/SQ special immigrants, religious workers and other statutory immigrant classes.Other immigrant visa processing: generally $205 per person; DV application fee: $330 per person when applicable.
↗ DV Program↗ Form I-360

🔵 Nonimmigrant Visas

Temporary travel, study, work, exchange and other authorized stays.
B, C, D, F, I, J, M, N, TN/TD, CWVisitors/business/tourism, transit, crewmembers, students, media, exchange visitors, vocational students and Canadian/Mexican professionals/dependents.Most non-petition-based categories: $185 visa application fee.
↗ DS-160 Online↗ Visa Directory
H, L, O, P, Q, RTemporary workers/trainees, intracompany transferees, extraordinary ability, athletes/artists/entertainers, cultural exchange and religious workers.DOS petition-based visa application fee: $205. USCIS Form I-129 H-1B: $780 paper / $730 online for regular petitioners, plus applicable additional fees.
↓ Form I-129↗ DS-160 Online
E, K, S, T, U, V and other temporary/special classesTreaty trader/investor and Australian professional; fiancé(e)/spouse-related K; witnesses/informants; trafficking/crime victims; V spouses/children of LPRs.E: $315. K: $265. S/T/U categories listed by DOS: $185 visa application fee where applicable. Reciprocity or other fees may also apply.
↗ Current DOS Fees↗ Form I-914↗ Form I-918

🟣 Other / Special Categories

Diplomatic, international-organization, NATO, border and special immigration classifications.
A, G, NATO and other official classificationsDiplomatic/government officials, representatives of international organizations and qualifying NATO personnel.DOS visa application fee: No Fee for qualifying A, G and NATO applicants.
↗ Official Visa Info
Border Crossing Card / MexicoBorder Crossing Card (BCC) for qualifying Mexican citizens.$185 for applicants age 15 and over; $15 for qualifying applicants under age 15.
↗ BCC Information
Humanitarian & Protection-Related FormsExamples include asylum/refugee-related filings and T/U humanitarian classifications. These are immigration benefits and may not be visa applications.Fees depend on the specific form and current USCIS fee schedule; some humanitarian forms are $0.
↗ USCIS Forms↗ Fee Schedule
Official-source note: Department of State visa fees are separate from USCIS petition/application fees. Reciprocity/issuance fees can vary by nationality, and medical, biometrics, SEVIS, affidavit-of-support, or other case-specific costs may apply. USCIS states that its forms are free to download; always use the latest official form edition and current fee schedule before filing.
